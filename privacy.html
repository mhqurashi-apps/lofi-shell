Privacy Policy

Lofi Shell · last updated 14 November 2026

The short version. Lofi Shell is local-first. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no backend of ours. Your data stays on your device unless you turn on a feature that connects somewhere: your servers, your AI endpoint, or your own iCloud account.

What stays on your device

Hosts, ports, usernames, folders and tags, known host keys, snippets, port forwards, themes, settings, command history (if enabled), session logs and AI history are stored in the app on this device and removed when you delete the app.

Servers and keys

Lofi Shell connects directly to the SSH, Mosh, SFTP and Git servers that you configure. Those connections are not routed through us: there is no intermediary service. Passwords, private keys and passphrases are stored in the iOS Keychain or Android Keystore. Generated keys are not additionally encrypted by the app.

AI assistant

AI is off unless you add an endpoint and key. When you ask a question, your prompt and, if enabled, recent terminal context are sent to the endpoint you configured. Your provider's privacy policy applies. We never receive that data.

Files and notifications

Files shared into the app are copied to the app cache and deleted after the upload. SFTP transfers go to the server you chose; previews use temporary storage on the device. On iOS, the first connection to a host on your local network asks for local network access. Terminal alerts and monitor thresholds use local notifications; there is no push server.

iCloud sync and the app lock

Both are off until you turn them on. With iCloud sync on, your hosts, folders, snippets and settings are written by Apple to your own iCloud account; we never see them and no password, private key or passphrase is ever synced. With the app lock on, the device itself checks Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode before the terminal opens. The check happens on the device and no biometric data leaves it.

Purchases

Lofi Shell downloads free and sells a Pro tier through the store. Payments are handled by Apple or Google: we never see your card details or your payment history. The app only learns which product you own and, for the yearly plan, when it renews, and it keeps that on your device.

Your choices

Delete a host to remove its keychain entries, turn off optional features such as command history, session logs, the AI assistant or iCloud sync, or uninstall the app. Because there is no account, there is nothing for us to delete remotely. The store remains the source of truth for a purchase.

Children

Lofi Shell is a developer tool and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes and the new text is published here and ships with an app update.
